01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project explores the development of a DIY projection system using repurposed components from an existing projector and a salvaged smartphone LCD panel.

The physical build brings together a dedicated light source, an LCD display assembly, custom optical components, and a cooling arrangement to produce a projected image on a viewing surface.

CORE IDEA

Repurpose available hardware and combine it with a custom optical and mechanical arrangement to create a functional DIY projector.

A visual walkthrough of the DIY smartphone projector prototype, showcasing the physical build, optical setup, and sample projected output.

03 / HARDWARE FRAMEWORK

Salvaged LCD Panel

A compact LCD panel recovered from an older Android smartphone serves as the image display component of the projector.

LED Light Source

A repurposed projector LED provides the illumination needed for the LCD display assembly and optical system.

Power and Control

Repurposed projector electronics form part of the power and control arrangement for the lighting and cooling components.

Physical Integration

The main components are brought together into a practical projector assembly designed around the available hardware.

04 / OPTICAL ALIGNMENT

The optical assembly uses multiple Fresnel lens elements arranged to manage light distribution and direct the projected image toward the viewing surface.

A separate field lens and projection lens arrangement completes the optical path, with attention given to image framing, focus, and edge clarity.

OPTICAL DESIGN

The custom optical arrangement is intended to improve light distribution and produce a more consistent projected image across the viewing area.

05 / THERMAL MANAGEMENT

The physical build incorporates a dual-stage cooling arrangement to support airflow around the projector's internal components.

The cooling system is part of the overall hardware design, helping manage heat produced during projector operation.

06 / PROJECTION OUTPUT

The completed prototype is designed to project a large-format image onto a matte viewing surface. Its optical arrangement focuses on image framing, focus, and edge-to-edge clarity.

Image Clarity

The optical setup is designed to maintain clear image details and consistent projection across the visible frame.

Projection Area

The prototype is intended for a large projected viewing area, depending on the projection distance and surrounding lighting conditions.
