About the Developer

The Work
Behind the
Projects.

I'm the developer behind DISKARTE TECH PH. These projects are part of my own journey in technology and development.

I started working on Android modifications in 2015. My first project was the Sony Xperia ROM for Acei, followed by my CMX11 Optimized ROM for Cooperve. I shared both projects with the XDA Developers community.

After my early ROM projects, I continued studying and working. In 2020, the pandemic also affected my work. I returned to hands-on Android customization in 2022 with Android Into Walkman, where I modified existing applications and integrated Sony Music into a Walkman-inspired Android setup.

In 2023, I created a PC tutorial showing how I used ADB AppControl to manage selected preinstalled Android applications.

From November 2025 to June 2026, I developed PEP Engine, a projector software project that brought my experience in Android customization and software modification into another type of device.

DISKARTE TECH PH is where I document and present the projects I have worked on, the solutions I have developed, and the work behind them.

Every project has its own process, challenges, and results. This archive keeps that work together in one place.
