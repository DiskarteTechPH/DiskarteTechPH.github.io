01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

How Android Into Walkman was created.

Android Into Walkman is a Walkman-inspired Android customization project. It was developed by combining Sony Music with modified Android applications and supporting plugins to create a customized music experience on a smartphone.

The application modifications were made using APKTool M. The original Spotify Launcher project was renamed Walkman Launcher and used specifically for Sony Music integration. Spotify access remains part of that Sony Music setup.

Sony Wikipedia Extension was modified and renamed Browser. Sony YouTube Music was also modified and renamed Sony YouTube Music Video. These are separate application modifications with their own functions in the project.

Sony Music serves as the main music player. YouTube Music Vanced can share a selected music or video link to NewPipe through Android's Share menu. NewPipe then handles the shared link and provides audio or video download options. Supported downloaded audio files can be detected by Sony Music's library when scanned.

ADB AppControl is included as a PC tool for managing applications on a connected Android device through ADB. It is separate from APKTool M, which was used for modifying the APK files.

This page documents the project and provides the applications, plugins, and supporting resources used in the setup. For the actual PC installation and uninstallation procedures, watch the tutorial video above.
