01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Old Phone. New Music Experience.

I worked on modifying existing Android applications using APKTool M to create a Sony Music-centered experience. The Sony Music interface became the main music environment, with additional integrations for browser access and YouTube Music.

The goal was to bring different music-related functions together and give an old Android smartphone a Walkman-inspired experience.
